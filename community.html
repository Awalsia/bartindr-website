Safety

Community Guidelines

Updated 2 October 2026

Be authentic

Use accurate photos, experience and workplace information.

Zero tolerance

Objectionable content and abusive behaviour are not tolerated.

Be respectful

No harassment, discrimination, threats, bullying or sexual pressure.

Keep opportunities honest

No fake roles, misleading compensation or false qualifications.

Protect personal information

Do not request passwords, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive data.

No scams or spam

No advance-fee requests, gift cards, cryptocurrency schemes or repetitive unsolicited messages.

Appropriate content

No unlawful, violent, hateful, exploitative or sexually explicit content.

Safe meetings

Meet in suitable public or professional places and independently verify the person or venue.

Reporting

Open the safety menu on a profile or in Browse, or tap the three red vertical dots on a match or in the chat header, select a reason and send a report. Press and hold a message to report that message. For support, email awalsia25@gmail.com.

Blocking and removing a match

Block user and report removes the user from your feed, closes your matches and conversations, and alerts the moderation team. Remove match closes that match without reporting or suspending the other user.

Media review and privacy

Profile and chat images can be reviewed automatically by OpenAI only with your separate explicit permission. You can choose manual review instead, and change or withdraw permission from Profile → Account → AI moderation privacy. Images awaiting approval stay hidden from other users. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Enforcement

We review objectionable-content reports within 24 hours. Confirmed objectionable content is removed and the offending user is expelled. Text filtering and media review help prevent prohibited content from reaching other users.
