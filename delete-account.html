Account controls

Delete your Bartind'r account

Deletion is permanent and is intended to remove your profile, matches, messages and uploaded content, except limited data retained for legitimate legal or security reasons.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Bartind'r and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open the Account menu.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the address associated with your account.

awalsia25@gmail.com
Subject: Bartind'r account deletion request

Verification

We may ask you to verify control of the account email. Never send your password.

What is deleted

  • Authentication account and profile.
  • Photos, videos and documents.
  • Likes, matches and messages.
  • Push tokens and account-specific status data.

Timing

Verified requests are processed without unreasonable delay. Secure backups may retain data temporarily until normal deletion cycles complete.
