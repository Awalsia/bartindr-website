Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Bartind'r processes information when you use the mobile app and website.

1. Controller and contact

Bartind'r is operated by Stefano Fabiano. Contact: awalsia25@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

We may process email address, role, name, city, country, biography, experience, skills, hourly rates, workplace details, photos, videos, documents, likes, matches, messages, reactions, read status, typing status, online status, last-seen data, push tokens, authentication records and technical logs. For safety, we also process terms acceptance and its timestamp, blocks, reports, reported content snapshots, moderation decisions and review notes.

3. Purposes

  • Create and secure accounts.
  • Display profiles and enable discovery, likes and matches.
  • Provide chat, media sharing and notifications.
  • Prevent abuse and respond to support requests.
  • Meet legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Where applicable, processing is based on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent and legal obligations.

5. Providers

Bartind'r uses service providers such as Supabase for authentication, database and storage, and Expo-related services for app infrastructure and push notifications. Resend delivers transactional email alerts to the moderation team. Images uploaded to profiles and chats, including images awaiting review, are sent to OpenAI for automated visual safety moderation only after you give separate explicit permission in the app. Chat text, videos, audio and documents are not sent to OpenAI by this feature. You may choose manual moderation instead; images remain hidden from other users until approved. Change or withdraw permission in Profile → Account → AI moderation privacy. Withdrawal stops future AI processing and does not reverse analysis already completed. Your choice and its timestamp are recorded for privacy compliance. Images that cannot be approved automatically remain in the moderator review queue; video, audio and documents require manual review.

6. Sharing

Profile information may be visible to eligible users. Messages and attachments are visible to match participants, subject to blocking and moderation. Authorised moderators can access pending media and private report evidence to investigate abuse. The reported user cannot access another user's private report through the app.

7. Retention

Data is kept while the account is active and for a limited period when needed for security, backups, disputes or legal compliance.

8. Deletion

Delete your account in the app through Profile → Account → Delete account, or use the web deletion page.

8.1 Images showing faces — no facial recognition

Bartind'r may collect profile and chat photos or videos showing faces, and an optional identity-document photo and a current selfie for identity verification. Bartind'r does not use facial recognition or create biometric templates, facial geometry maps or facial identifiers. These images are not used by Bartind'r for advertising profiling or biometric model training.

8.2 Optional manual identity verification

If you request the Verified badge, an authorised administrator manually compares your selfie with your identity document. We do not perform an automated facial comparison. Identity-document images and verification selfies are not public, are not shown to other users and are not sent to OpenAI by the identity-verification workflow. Review notifications contain request details rather than copies of the images.

8.3 Storage, access and providers

Images are stored through Supabase. Our production project is in the eu-west-1 region (Ireland). Identity documents and verification selfies are in a private Storage bucket. Authorised administrators access them through temporary signed links. Profile and chat images follow the visibility rules in section 6.

With separate explicit permission, profile and chat images may be submitted to OpenAI's Moderation API to screen for prohibited content, not to identify people. Identity-verification documents and selfies are excluded from that workflow. Declining or withdrawing permission selects manual moderation for future processing. OpenAI's processing and retention rules are described in its API data controls documentation.

8.4 Image retention and deletion

Identity-document images and verification selfies are retained while the verification request is pending and are automatically deleted from active Storage after 7 days from approval or rejection. The cleanup job checks for expired files every 15 minutes. The verification result, timestamps and Verified badge may remain while the account is active; these records do not contain the deleted images or a biometric template.

Deleting a profile photo or video through Manage media requests removal of that file from active Storage. Hiding a chat or removing a match does not delete its attachments. Chat attachments remain associated with the conversation until removed by moderation or deletion of the owning account. Account deletion also removes any remaining identity-verification files associated with that account. A report or moderation record may retain text, decisions and references to content; it does not preserve a separate copy of a file removed from Storage.

Bartind'r does not maintain separate backups of identity-document images or verification selfies. Supabase database backups contain database records and file metadata, not the Storage files themselves; restoring such a database backup does not restore a deleted image. Provider processing and any applicable retention exceptions follow the providers' published terms. This is distinct from the active-Storage deletion periods described above.

9. Rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing.

10. Children

Bartind'r is intended for users aged 18 or older.

11. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes.
