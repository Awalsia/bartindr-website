This policy explains how Bartind'r processes information when you use the mobile app and website.

We may process email address, role, name, city, country, biography, experience, skills, hourly rates, workplace details, photos, videos, documents, likes, matches, messages, reactions, read status, typing status, online status, last-seen data, push tokens, authentication records and technical logs.

  • Create and secure accounts.

4. Legal bases

Where applicable, processing is based on contract performance, legitimate interests, consent and legal obligations.

5. Providers

Bartind'r uses service providers such as Supabase for authentication, database and storage, and Expo-related services for app infrastructure and push notifications.

Profile information may be visible to eligible users. Messages and attachments are visible to match participants.

7. Retention

Data is kept while the account is active and for a limited period when needed for security, backups, disputes or legal compliance.

8. Deletion

Delete your account in the app through Profile → Account → Delete account, or use the web deletion page.

9. Rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing.
